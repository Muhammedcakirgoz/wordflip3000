# Privacy Policy - WordFlip 3000

**Last Updated**: January 21, 2026

## 1. Introduction

WordFlip 3000 ("App") values the privacy of its users. This privacy policy explains what information is collected when using the app, how it is used, and how it is protected.

## 2. Information Collection

### 2.1 Personal Information
**WordFlip 3000 does NOT collect any personal information.**

Our app:
- Does NOT collect name, email, or phone number
- Does NOT collect location data
- Does NOT access contacts
- Does NOT access camera or microphone

### 2.2 Usage Data
Information stored locally on your device:
- Words you've learned and your progress
- Correct/incorrect answer statistics
- Your preferred settings (theme, language, etc.)
- Daily goals and challenge status

**Important**: This data is stored ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE and is never sent to any server.

## 3. Data Usage

Locally stored data is used only for:
- Tracking your learning progress
- Providing personalized learning experience
- Remembering your app preferences
- Displaying your statistics

## 4. Third-Party Services

### 4.1 Google ML Kit (Translation)
The app uses Google's ML Kit Translate feature:
- Translation models are **downloaded to your device**
- Translation happens **offline**
- Translated texts are NOT sent to Google

More about ML Kit:
https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

### 4.2 Other Services
- NO advertising services
- NO analytics services
- NO social media integration
- NO third-party trackers

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Local Storage
- All data is stored in your device's secure internal storage
- Data is not encrypted (because it contains no sensitive information)
- Other apps cannot access your data

### 5.2 Internet Connection
- App works **offline**
- Internet connection NOT required
- NO data transfer (except translation model download)

## 6. Children's Privacy

WordFlip 3000 is suitable for ages 3 and up. The app:
- Does NOT knowingly collect information from children
- Complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
- Does not require age verification (because no data is collected)

## 7. User Rights

### 7.1 Data Deletion
You can delete your data at any time:
- Settings → Data Reset → Reset All Progress
- Or uninstall the app from your device

### 7.2 Data Portability
Since data is local:
- Automatic backup is disabled
- Data does not transfer when changing devices
- Export/import feature is not available

## 8. Cookies and Tracking

**WordFlip 3000 does NOT use cookies or tracking technologies.**
- No analytics cookies
- No advertising cookies
- No session tracking

## 9. Permissions

The app uses the following permissions:

### Used Permissions
- **Storage (Internal)**: To store your learning data
- **Vibration** (Optional): For haptic feedback

### NOT Used Permissions
- ❌ Location
- ❌ Camera
- ❌ Microphone
- ❌ Contacts
- ❌ Phone
- ❌ SMS
